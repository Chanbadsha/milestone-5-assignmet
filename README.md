# Save Bangladesh - Donation Platform

**Project Overview**

Save Bangladesh is a donation platform dedicated to providing urgent relief to those affected by recent crises in Bangladesh. This includes:

* **Flood Relief:** Supporting victims in severely impacted areas like Noakhali and Feni with essential resources such as food, clean water, shelter, and medical supplies.
* **Quota Movement Support:** Providing medical aid and support to individuals injured during the recent protests.

**Technology Stack**

* HTML

**Main Features**

* **Clear and concise information:** Detailed descriptions of each cause, highlighting the urgent need for support.
* **Multiple donation options:** Flexible donation amounts and secure payment gateways for easy and convenient contributions.
* **User-friendly interface:** Intuitive navigation and a visually appealing design to encourage engagement and donations.
* **Transparency:** Clear information on how donations are used and the impact they have on the affected communities.

**Mission**

Save Bangladesh aims to unite individuals and organizations in their efforts to support those in need during these challenging times. By providing a simple and accessible platform for donations, we strive to foster resilience and empower communities to recover from these crises.

We urge you to join us in this critical effort. Every contribution, no matter how small, makes a significant difference. Please donate today and help Save Bangladesh.
